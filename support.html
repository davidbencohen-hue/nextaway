Common questions

The widget does not show my trip. Open Tilday once, then touch and hold the widget, choose Edit Widget and pick the trip.

How do I import trips from my calendar? On the trip list, open the menu and choose the calendar import. Allow calendar access when asked.

How do I cancel Tilday Supporter? Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Tilday.

How do I restore a purchase? Open the Tip Jar in the app and tap Restore Purchases.
