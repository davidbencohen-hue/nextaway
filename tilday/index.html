Tilday

Countdowns to trips, birthdays and big days, for iPhone and iPad.

Tilday counts the days to whatever you are looking forward to, in the app and in widgets on your Home Screen. Each countdown gets a photo, and a trip gets a plan: its flights, where you are staying, lists, notes and tickets in one place.

What it does

  • Countdowns and widgets. Add a trip or a day by typing, by voice, or from your calendar. Pick a widget style for your Home Screen.
  • A plan for each trip. Keep flights, stays, checklists, notes, links and photos with the trip they belong to.
  • Bookings from Gmail (optional). If you choose to connect your Gmail account, Tilday looks in your mailbox for travel confirmations that match a trip's dates, such as flights, hotels, rentals and tickets, and offers them for you to add to that trip's plan with one tap.

How Tilday uses Gmail

Connecting Gmail is optional and is done with Google's own sign-in. Tilday asks for read-only access to your Gmail (gmail.readonly) for one purpose: to find your travel booking confirmations and show them on the trip they belong to.

  • The search and the reading happen in the app on your own device. Emails go straight from Google to your device.
  • Tilday has no server. The developer never receives, sees or stores your email.
  • Email text is not kept. Only a booking you choose to add is saved, with your trip.
  • Your Gmail data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never used to train AI models.
  • You can disconnect at any time in the app, which removes Tilday's access at Google.

The full details are in the Privacy Policy.
