Privacy Policy

Tilday for iPhone and iPad · Effective November 26, 2026

The short version

Tilday does not collect, store or sell your personal information. There are no Tilday accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking. The developer has no server. Your countdowns, and anything read from your calendar or your Gmail, stay on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your countdowns and trips. Names, dates, plans and photos you choose are saved on your device. The developer cannot see them.
  • Your calendar. If you allow calendar access, Tilday reads your events on the device so you can turn them into countdowns. Calendar data is not sent anywhere.
  • Your Gmail, if you connect it. See the next section.

Gmail and Google user data

Connecting a Gmail account is optional. If you connect one, this is exactly what happens.

  • What Tilday accesses. You sign in with Google's own sign-in screen and grant Tilday read-only access to your Gmail (the gmail.readonly permission), plus your email address so the app can show which account is connected. Tilday cannot send, change or delete email.
  • How it is used. For a trip, the app on your device searches your mailbox for travel booking confirmations (flights, places to stay, rental cars, trains and tickets) and reads the matching messages to find the booking's details: what it is, its dates and times, the place and the confirmation code. Those details are shown on the trip as suggestions. That is the only use.
  • Where it is processed. Entirely on your device. Messages travel directly from Google to your device over an encrypted connection and are read in memory by the app, including by Apple's on-device language model where your device has it. Nothing from your Gmail is sent to the developer, because there is no developer server, and nothing is sent to any other company or service.
  • What is stored. Email text is not stored. When you tap to add a suggested booking, its details are saved with your trip like anything else you add to it. Your Google sign-in is kept in the device's Keychain, on that device only.
  • Sharing. Tilday does not share, transfer, sell or disclose Google user data to anyone. No person at the developer can read your email, because it never leaves your device.
  • Never used for. Advertising of any kind, building profiles, or creating, training or improving artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • Removing access and deleting data. Choose Disconnect Gmail in the app (the menu on the "From your Gmail" card of any trip). That revokes Tilday's access at Google and deletes the saved sign-in from the device. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Bookings you added stay on your trips until you delete them there; deleting the app deletes everything it saved on the device.

Tilday's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What leaves your device

  • Photo searches. To find a picture for a countdown, the app sends the search words (usually the destination name) to photo services: Unsplash, Pexels, Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons. Like any internet request, this includes your IP address. Those services handle the request under their own privacy policies.
  • Voice input. If you tap the microphone, your speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition, which may process audio on Apple's servers under Apple's privacy policy. Tilday does not keep recordings.
  • Purchases. Tips and the Tilday Supporter subscription are handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees your payment details.
  • Google, if you connect Gmail. Signing in and reading your mailbox are requests from your device to Google, under Google's privacy policy.

Sharing trips

When you share trips, Tilday makes a link that holds each trip's name, dates and plan titles. Notes, photos and booking details such as confirmation codes are not included. The trips are inside the link itself and are not stored on a server; anyone you give the link to can read them.

Children

Tilday does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
