Privacy Policy

Tilday for iPhone · Effective November 24, 2026

The short version

Tilday does not collect, store or sell your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking. Your trips stay on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your trips. Trip names, dates and photos you choose are saved on your iPhone only. The developer has no server and cannot see them.
  • Your calendar. If you allow calendar access, Tilday reads your events on the device so you can turn them into trips. Calendar data is not sent anywhere.

What leaves your device

  • Photo searches. To find a picture for a trip, the app sends the search words (usually the destination name) to photo services: Unsplash, Pexels, Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons. Like any internet request, this includes your IP address. Those services handle the request under their own privacy policies.
  • Voice input. If you tap the microphone, your speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition, which may process audio on Apple's servers under Apple's privacy policy. Tilday does not keep recordings.
  • Purchases. Tips and the Tilday Supporter subscription are handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees your payment details.

Children

Tilday does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Sharing trips

When you share trips, Tilday makes a link that holds each trip's name, dates and plan. Notes and photos are not included. The trips are inside the link itself and are not stored on a server; anyone you give the link to can read them.
